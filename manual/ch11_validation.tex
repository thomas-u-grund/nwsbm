\chapter{Validation against the R packages}\label{ch:val}

This chapter reports what the package was checked against; the checks are part of the test suite of the package and were run on macOS with Stata 17 (the test suite also covers recovery of planted partitions and error paths). Test networks were simulated in R with fixed seeds; the references are \sbmR{} 1.0.0 and \blockmodels{} 1.2.1 (which \sbmR{} calls), and \greed{} 0.6.2. In each comparison the memberships are compared by the adjusted Rand index (ARI) after matching the blocks.

\paragraph{Deviations that are not errors.} For the Bernoulli two-mode model with covariates the ICL cannot be compared with \blockmodels{}, because its PL exceeds the maximal log likelihood (the coefficient is 0.012 apart). The R covariate models treat missing ties of Bernoulli and Poisson networks as zeros, which \nwsbm{} does not, so fixtures with missing ties are not compared there. Away from the selected number of blocks the two programs can end in different local optima. The standard errors, \opt{blockvar}, \opt{sigma2()} and \opt{orderblocks} have no R reference; they were checked against the direct computations named in the table.

\paragraph{Recovery of planted partitions.} In networks of 100 actors with three planted blocks and a between-block tie probability of 0.10, the memberships are recovered perfectly from a within-block probability of 0.5 (undirected) and nearly perfectly (directed) from 0.3; below about 0.2 no method recovers the blocks. The Poisson, Gaussian, degree-corrected and two-mode models select the planted number of blocks with an ARI of at least 0.90 on the simulated networks of the test suite (the degree-corrected one with strongly heterogeneous degrees reaches 0.90; the plain Poisson model splits such networks into too many blocks).
